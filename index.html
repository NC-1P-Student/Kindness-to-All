Cyberbullying can hurt people, even when it happens on a screen. Everyone deserves respect, kindness and support.

Learn the signs Get help

What is cyberbullying?

It is not okay to be mean online.

📱

Mean comments

Bullying can happen in texts, games, social media, chats and videos.

😢

Rumours

Sharing hurtful messages or embarrassing pictures can make someone feel lonely and scared.

🛑

Repeated behaviour

If someone keeps targeting another person online, it is bullying even if it happens on a screen.

How to spot it

Some signs that someone may be hurt online.

😟

Upset

They seem sad, worried or quiet after being online.

🚫

Avoiding devices

They stop using games or chats or avoid their phone.

😵

Stress

They seem tired, anxious or unable to focus.

💬

Hidden messages

They hide their screen or delete messages quickly.

What can a child do?

Helpful steps when online bullying happens.

  • Save screenshots or messages.
  • Do not reply with mean words.
  • Block the person if needed.
  • Tell a trusted adult, teacher or family member.
  • Ask a safe grown-up to help report it.

Spread kindness

Small kind actions make a big difference.

😊

Say something nice in a chat or game.

🤝

Include someone who is being left out.

📣

Report mean messages instead of sharing them.

💛

Listen, support and be a friend.

Remember

You are not alone.

If someone is hurting you online, tell a trusted adult. You deserve to feel safe, respected and included.
